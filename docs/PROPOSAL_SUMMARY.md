# Chaubandi — Proposal Summary (Locked Understanding)

_Source: `Chaubandi_Proposal.pptx` (44 slides) · Prepared by Srii (Web & Digital Partner) for Sushma (Founder). Locked 2026-06-19._

## The decision the proposal makes
Build a **custom headless online store** (not Shopify), run **done-for-you** end-to-end. Shopify is acknowledged to win on speed-to-launch and hands-off upkeep; the custom build is chosen for **live video shopping, saved measurements, and physical-store integration** (Slide 13).

## Core strategy
- **Problem being solved:** two fears that stop people buying expensive ethnic wear online — "Will it fit?" and "Will it look like the photo?" (Slide 5).
- **Unfair advantages:** real Arlington store, a founder who styles, 200K IG followers, free alterations & shipping. Honest caveat: 200K followers = attention, not revenue; the site's job is converting it (Slide 6).

## What gets built — 4 layers (Slides 14–23)
1. **Foundation (table stakes):** product catalogue, cart + Stripe checkout, customer accounts, founder's story, Google Reviews, maps + free-service messaging.
2. **Signature features (the moat):**
   - **Live Video Shopping** — private 1:1 video session, **$10 fee** (filters serious buyers, premium feel, small income).
   - **Saved Measurements** — stored once, reused for every order; attacks the fit fear + drives reorders.
3. **Phase-1 enhancements (finalised):**
   - **"Design Your Dream Outfit"** — inspiration-photo upload → custom-design request → draft order Sushma prices/confirms; photo stored, Sushma emailed instantly. ≈ $0 software.
   - **"Find Your Perfect Fit"** — two-photo (front + side) + height → full measurements, **96–97% accuracy**; human confirmation + free alterations as safety nets. **The one real subscription (quote-based); free lower-accuracy fallback exists.**
   - **Store Pickup (BOPIS)** — ship or pick up free in Arlington / reserve to try on. $0, native.
4. **Future roadmap (not committed):** live shopping events, rental tier, bridal group ordering, AI occasion stylist, get-found-by-AI-search, 3D/360 views.

## Tech stack (Slide 22)
Engine: **Medusa.js** · Storefront: **React** · Hosting: **Railway + Vercel** · Storage: **Google Cloud** · Payments: **Stripe** (standard fee, no surcharge) · Email/social proof: **SendGrid + Google Maps/Reviews**.

## Investment (Slides 26–32)
- **One-time build: $5,700** = $4,000 website dev + $1,700 photography & upload of all current stock.
- **Monthly retainer: $1,800** = $700 weekly photo/uploads + $550 social (8 videos + 12–16 photos/mo, IG+FB) + $350 basic ad management + $200 maintenance.
- **Infra pass-through: $50–115/mo** (Railway+Vercel hosting, storage) + domain ≈$15/yr + **Stripe ≈2.9% + 30¢/sale** + ad spend (owner's budget).
- **Payment terms:** 50% deposit ($2,850) to start · 50% on launch · retainer billed monthly.

## Excluded / extra cost (Slide 31)
Large ad budgets/advanced campaigns, professional model photoshoots, long-form description writing, logo/brand identity & major redesigns.

## Working together (Slides 34–36)
- **Srii:** photography & uploads, social content, ads, maintenance, reporting, technical support.
- **Sushma:** flag new stock, reply to comments/DMs, orders & fulfilment, returns/exchanges (existing in-store process), ad budget & approvals, promo ideas.

## Launch plan (Slides 37–42)
Rule: **zero spend before launch.** Pre-launch (4 wks: collect contacts in-store, BTS content, countdown, DM regulars, announce date) → Launch week (early access, IG announce, daily Stories, convert in-store visits) → First 30 days ($0 organic; optional $300–500 ads only if organic is slow). **Offers:** free alterations (first 50 — hero), 10% off first order, free shipping >$300, launch gift, refer-a-friend (10%/10%).

## Open items the proposal leaves for the meeting (basis for the questionnaire)
Catalog volume & data handover, exact measurement schema + privacy/consent, $10-fee mechanics & calendar, custom-request pricing/turnaround SLA, payment methods beyond cards, tax/shipping/returns rules to encode, fulfilment automation, the measurement-subscription vendor choice (only recurring software cost), Google Reviews source, email-tool ownership, and which roadmap item is the first Phase-2 follow-on.
